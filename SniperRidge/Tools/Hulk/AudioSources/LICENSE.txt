roar_02.wav and grunt_03.wav are mono PCM conversions of the original Ogg files.
The author describes creating most sounds with their mouth and applying filters.
The game transformation cue combines and pitch-shifts these recordings with EQ,
short room reflections and a bounded peak level. No film/game voice was sampled.
Rebuild using Tools/Hulk/build_transform_roar.py (Python + NumPy).
